# Karpathy Pattern: Operating Principles

Adapted from [Karpathy’s LLM Wiki gist](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f), read 2026-10-14. Applies to both tool and manual routes.

- Maintain accumulated understanding: connect each source to existing topics and revise the broader synthesis, beyond creating isolated summaries.
- Keep three concerns distinct: immutable evidence, editable knowledge, and evolving operating conventions. Follow the actual vault’s schema.
- Let the user direct scope and emphasis. Support involved, one-source sessions or authorized batches without imposing either.
- During ingestion, check affected entities, concepts, comparisons, and overview pages. Preserve competing claims with citations.
- Answer from linked evidence; retain useful analyses when authorized so discoveries remain reusable.
- Distinguish the content catalog from chronological activity history. Managed vaults delegate both to tooling; standalone wikis maintain their own files.
- Health reviews should identify missing connections, unsupported or stale claims, and researchable gaps; suggest the next useful question.
- Inspect referenced images separately when they carry evidence. Search engines, graphs, and presentation formats remain optional aids.
